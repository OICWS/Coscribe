Build a small profit model in a new workbook budget_model.xlsx, one sheet called Model, laid out like this:

- A2:B5 are the inputs (label in A, value in B): Units sold 1000, Price per unit 25, Cost per unit 14, Fixed costs 6000.
- A7:B11 are the results: Revenue (units times price), Variable costs (units times cost per unit), Gross profit (revenue minus variable costs), Net profit (gross profit minus fixed costs), Net margin (net profit divided by revenue, shown as a percentage).

The results are formulas on the inputs, so that changing any input in B2:B5 recalculates everything. Use the labels exactly as written above.
