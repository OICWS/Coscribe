shop.xlsx has two sheets: Orders (Order ID, SKU, Qty) and Prices (SKU, Product, Unit price). In Orders add three columns next to the existing ones: D = Product, E = Unit price, F = Line total (unit price times quantity), each looked up from Prices by SKU for every order row with a formula. Put the label Total in E11 and the sum of the line totals in F11. Leave the existing cells and the Prices sheet as they are. If a price changes in Prices, Orders must follow.
