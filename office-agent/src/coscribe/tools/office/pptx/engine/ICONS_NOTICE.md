`icons.json` holds 293 icons of Tabler Icons v3.46.0 (outline; commit
`8ac7d81b72ece11072ef25ea9fd92e80c6f3c9fc`), taken from the snapshot ppt-master bundles
(`hugohe3/ppt-master`, `skills/ppt-master/templates/icons/tabler-outline/`, commit `8070d3d`).
Each entry keeps the icon's tags and its drawn elements on the 24 x 24 grid; nothing in the
geometry is changed.
