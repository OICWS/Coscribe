---
title: Plans and task lists
description: Review a plan before a large job starts, change its steps, and follow progress in the task list.
---

For a job with many steps or files, or one that is hard to undo, coscribe writes a plan first and waits for you. In **Plan** mode it does this before any change at all.

## Review a plan

The plan arrives as a card in the conversation and opens in the **Plan** panel beside it. The panel shows the plan, and under it the steps coscribe intends to take. While the plan waits, you can edit the steps there:

- Untick a step to leave it out.
- Click a step to reword it.
- Move a step up or down, or delete it.
- Add a step; <kbd>Enter</kbd> in the last step adds the next one.

The card says when you changed the list, and coscribe carries out your version.

## Answer the plan

- **Accept** starts the work in the current mode.
- **Accept and use auto mode** starts the work and lets a reviewer model approve routine actions for the rest of it. <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd> does the same.
- **Revise…** sends the plan back with what you want changed.

## Follow the task list

Once work starts, the task list shows each step. A finished step gets a tick and is struck through, and a long list shows five steps at a time. The **Task details** panel shows the whole list.
