---
title: Your first conversation
description: Describe a task in a sentence, watch Coscribe work, and decide at the points that matter.
---

Once a provider is added, describe a task in the message box on the home screen.

## Describe a task

Write it as you would for a colleague: what you need, from which material, and in what form. For example:

> Remove the orders in `Q3 orders.xlsx` that have no shipping address, total the amounts by supplier, and save the result as a new Excel file.

Drag the files into the window, or click **+** at the bottom left of the message box to attach them; you can also just name a file and Coscribe looks for it in the workspace. Press <kbd>Enter</kbd> to send and <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Enter</kbd> for a new line.

## Watch it work

Coscribe reads the material first, then works step by step. A larger task starts with a task list, and each item is struck through when it is done. Finished files appear above the message box; click one to preview it.

You don't have to wait while it works:

- **Add to the request**: keep typing in the message box; it reads your note at its next step.
- **Stop it**: click the stop button at the right of the message box. What is already done is kept.

## Permission modes

A mode button below the message box decides whether Coscribe asks you before it acts:

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Manual** | Asks before every change, script or outside action. |
| **Accept Edits** | Edits files in your folders without asking; still asks before running code or outside actions. |
| **Plan** | Researches and writes a plan for you to approve before any change. |
| **Auto** | A reviewer model approves routine actions and blocks risky ones instead of asking you. |

When your approval is needed, a card in the conversation says what Coscribe wants to do. Read it, then click **Approve** or **Deny**.

> [!TIP]
> When you start out, choose **Manual** or **Plan**. Switch to **Accept Edits** or **Auto** once you know how Coscribe works.

In every mode, Coscribe writes only to the workspace and the other folders you allowed.

## Commands and shortcuts

Type <kbd>/</kbd> in the message box for a list of commands, such as compacting a conversation or making a copy of it. Press <kbd>?</kbd>, or click the keyboard icon at the top right, to see every keyboard shortcut.
