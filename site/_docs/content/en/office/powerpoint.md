---
title: PowerPoint decks
description: Turn material into a presentation, on your own template or design system, and revise decks without losing your edits.
---

coscribe makes `.pptx` files you can open and edit in PowerPoint or WPS: real text, shapes, tables, native charts, pictures, icons, speaker notes, animations and transitions.

## Make a new deck

Describe the audience and what the deck should say, and attach the material, such as a spreadsheet or a report. For example:

> Make a 7-slide quarterly review for the leadership meeting from `Q3 sales.xlsx`: summary, monthly trend, regions, product mix, customers, risks and the Q4 plan.

coscribe designs each slide, builds the deck, then renders the slides and looks at them to check the layout before giving it to you.

## Use your own look

- **Your template**: attach a `.pptx` template and ask coscribe to use it. It reads the template's layouts, colors and fonts.
- **A design system**: save your colors, fonts, logo and pictures once under **Settings › Design systems**, and ask for a deck "in our design system". See [Design systems](../customize/design-systems.md).

## Change an existing deck

coscribe can edit any slide: change text, tables and charts, replace or crop pictures, add or remove slides, reorder them, change the theme colors, add speaker notes, animations and transitions.

When you ask it to revise a deck it made, it changes that deck in place. If you edited the file yourself in PowerPoint since, coscribe keeps your edits; when it can't, it asks before replacing them. Ask for a "new version" if you want a separate file.
