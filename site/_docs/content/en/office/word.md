Coscribe writes real `.docx` files: headings, tables, pictures, native charts, a table of contents, comments and tracked changes.

## Word file or Doc?

For writing you mainly want to read and edit in Coscribe, it makes a **Doc**, its own editable document shown beside the conversation (see [Docs, designs and dashboards](artifacts.md)). Ask for **Word** or a **.docx** when you need a Word file: to send it, to use your template, or to track changes.

## Preview a Word file

Open a `.docx` attachment or select one in **Files** to read the saved document page by page. Use **Fit width** or a zoom level, and **Open in Word** to edit the original. Coscribe creates the preview locally using Microsoft Word, WPS, or LibreOffice. The first preview may take a moment.

The preview follows the saved file. If you save it in Word or Coscribe changes it,
the new pages appear at your current page and zoom when they are ready.

## Revise with tracked changes

Ask Coscribe to revise a document "with tracked changes" and each insertion and deletion is marked as in Word's Track Changes, with your comments where it explains a change. Your colleagues can then accept or reject each one in Word.

Coscribe can also read the tracked changes and comments already in a document, answer them, and accept or reject changes you name.

## Use your own template

Attach a `.docx` template, or name one in the workspace, and ask Coscribe to build on it. The new document starts from the template's styles and page setup.

## It checks the pages

After writing a Word file, Coscribe lays it out with Microsoft Word, WPS or LibreOffice (whichever is on your computer) and checks the pages: an empty page, a table or line that runs past the edge of the paper, or a last page with only a line or two. It sees what it finds, so it can fix the document before handing it to you.
