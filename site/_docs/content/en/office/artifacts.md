title: Docs, designs and dashboards

Besides Office files, coscribe makes **artifacts**: work that opens in a panel next to the conversation, where you read and edit it. Every artifact is listed on the **Artifacts** page.

| Artifact | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Doc** | An editable document for writing you want to keep, edit, comment on or send: a report, a proposal, a plan, a letter. coscribe makes a Doc unless you ask for Word. |
| **Slides** | A deck shown slide by slide beside the conversation. |
| **Design** | A canvas of boards: screens, a poster, a social image, a logo sheet, several options side by side. |
| **Dashboard** | A page of your real data, with figures, charts, a table and filters, that can be refreshed when the data changes. |

## Present and export a design

On a design's canvas, **Present** fills the panel with the boards one after another; use the arrow keys or **Previous** and **Next**, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> to go back. Above each board, **Present from here** starts with that board and **Download PNG** saves it as a picture.

## Change an artifact

Edit an artifact in its panel, or ask in the conversation. When you change a deck or a Doc yourself, coscribe is told what you changed at your next message, so it builds on your edits instead of undoing them.
