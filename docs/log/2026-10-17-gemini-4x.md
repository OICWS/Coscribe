# Gemini gets its effort levels back on langchain-google-genai 4.x (shipped)

Why: #402 item 5. #396 turned effort off for Gemini because 3.x sent an unknown `thinking_level`
field (#315). 4.x sends `reasoning_effort` as `thinking_config.thinking_level`.

## Shipped
- `pyproject.toml`: `langchain-google-genai>=4.3.0,<4.4`. 4.3 is the first with the
  `reasoning_effort` field; 4.4 requires `google-genai>=2.20`, past the `<2.14` range pinned for
  `Part.thought_signature`. `pip install -e ".[dev,web]"` resolves (4.3.7, google-genai 2.13.0).
- `runtime_lg/effort.py`: `ChatGoogleGenerativeAI` reads the levels from the library's model profile
  like Anthropic. `minimal` is not on our scale and is dropped: gemini-3.5-flash gets low, medium,
  high; gemini-3-pro-preview low, high (medium runs as low); 2.x has no profile levels and gets none.
- `tests/test_effort.py`: Gemini rows and request-param checks for `thinking_config`.

## Not verified
- Only gemini-3.5-flash was called live (two calls: low and max, which runs as high). Other Gemini
  models are covered by the library's profile, not by a call.
- Windows.

## Follow-ups
- Gemini 2.x takes `thinking_budget`; mapping levels to budgets was not done.
- Move to 4.4+ when the `thought_signature` pin on google-genai can be lifted.
