# Reasoning effort, part 2: the effort pill and slider next to the model (shipped)

Why: part 1 (#203) made the level something a conversation carries; the maintainer asked for the
control in the composer, between the model and the context ring, as Claude Code draws it (#202).

## Shipped
- `components/EffortPicker.tsx`: a pill with the level; its panel reads "Effort <level>", a ? with
  what effort trades off, Faster / Smarter, a six-stop track with a white thumb and the
  Recommended mark under Medium. Click, drag or the arrow keys change it; the choice is sent as
  `switch_model` with the same model and the new `effort`. The panel does not say which of the
  model's own levels a choice runs as (the maintainer, after trying it: the scale is the same for
  every model, the mapping stays behind it); a model with no effort setting shows the control
  disabled and says so.
- Ultra (`components/UltraField.tsx`, a canvas), matched to Claude Code's: a smooth field from
  grey at the faster end to a muted violet at the smarter end (colours measured on the
  maintainer's reference) sweeps in from the right in a second and then holds still; along its
  edge, three squares deep, each square fades in on its own, so the edge is ragged and uneven in
  shade; over it a grid of squares, five to the track's height, each a little lighter than the
  field, so the field draws the lines and the grid fades with it towards the left; white lights
  squares at random in soft waves that keep running right to left, two per 2.4 s. Under the
  system's reduced-motion setting the field shows still, without the waves. The label is violet.
  Earlier versions (a CSS gradient with sparks; a field where every square twinkled; squares too
  small; then too saturated, with dark lines) did not match the reference; this one was compared
  with it side by side.
- The slider shows a new level at once and counts the next step from it until the server confirms
  that same level: found live, quick key presses landed short, first because a step counted from
  the old level, then because the reply to an earlier step pulled the slider back. Four presses
  60 ms apart now land on Ultra. An error reply leaves no state, so a picked level is dropped
  after 3 seconds without confirmation.
- `state/reducer.ts` keeps `effort` and `effortRunsAs`; the reducer test checks them.
- Looked at live against a real backend (DeepSeek, no messages sent): Medium, High, and Ultra's
  sweep at 0.15, 0.4, 0.9, 2 and 2.6 s, with "This model runs it as Max".
