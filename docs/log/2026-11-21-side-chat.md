# Side chat: a question about the conversation, answered beside it (shipped)

Why: a quick question about a long conversation ("what does this figure mean?") would clutter it
and its context if asked there, and cannot be asked while a turn runs (#1023).

## Shipped
- A **Side chat** panel (two speech bubbles in the toolbar), a dock panel like Plan: "Coscribe
  sees this session. Nothing here is added to it." It works while a turn runs.
- `POST /api/threads/{id}/side-chat` (`web/routes/threads.py`) takes the side chat so far and
  answers with the conversation's own model, with no tools, from the conversation's transcript as
  it is now (`conversation/side_chat.py`, its newest 120,000 characters). It writes nothing: no
  checkpoint, no turn lock, nothing in the sidebar.
- The messages live only in the panel: closing it, another conversation or a reload drops them,
  since the answers were read against the conversation as it was. The panel has no "Open in new
  window", which would start it empty.
- Tests: `tests/test_side_chat.py`, two in `tests/web/test_thread_history.py`,
  `frontend/tests/e2e/side-chat.spec.ts` (passed here against the stub model).
- Tried with DeepSeek (`deepseek-flash`, effort low) while a turn wrote a long plan: it answered
  the owner and budget, answered a Chinese question in Chinese, said the transcript does not hold
  a per-week budget or what `shortlist.xlsx` contains, and the export afterwards held none of the
  side chat.
